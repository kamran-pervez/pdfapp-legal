Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EdifyPDF (“EdifyPDF,” “the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), provided by KK Innovation Labs, handles information when you use our mobile application on Android or iOS.

EdifyPDF is a PDF toolkit that helps you create, view, edit, convert, organize, sign, print, share, and secure PDF files. Core document processing is designed to run on your device. You do not need an EdifyPDF account to use the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Our Terms & Conditions also apply.

App: EdifyPDF
Android package: com.kkinnovationlabs.edifypdf
iOS bundle ID: com.edifypdf.app
Privacy contact: support.kklabs@gmail.com

2. Privacy highlights

  • PDFs, images, and related files you open or create are processed locally on your device for the feature you use.
  • We do not operate an EdifyPDF document cloud that uploads your files to our servers for conversion or editing.
  • The free App may show Google AdMob interstitial (full-screen) ads after successful tool use, subject to consent and frequency limits.
  • Where Firebase is configured for your platform, the App may send privacy-safe usage analytics, crash reports, and configuration fetches to Google Firebase by default. You can turn analytics and crash reporting off in Settings → Privacy (“Share Analytics”).
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • The App does not require a user login or EdifyPDF account.

3. Information we process

3.1 Your documents and media

When you use EdifyPDF features — including scan to PDF, images to PDF, import, view, edit, merge, extract, compress, rotate, reorder, delete pages, PDF to images, Word to PDF, password protect or unlock, sign, watermark, annotate, fill forms, print, share, and searchable PDF (OCR) — the App reads and writes files you select or create.

Those files stay under your control on the device and in storage locations you choose (for example Downloads, Files, or Photos). We do not use the contents of your documents to build advertising or marketing profiles about you.

Temporary working files may be created while a tool runs and are cleaned up in normal operation. Uninstalling the App removes App-private storage, subject to your operating system.

3.2 Passwords, signatures, and similar local assets

  • PDF passwords you enter to unlock or protect a file are used on the device for that operation. They are not intended to be uploaded to us or written to long-term storage by the App. Password-related temporary files are handled locally and should be deleted after use as part of normal tool flows.
  • Saved signatures / stamps you create may be stored locally in App storage (for example image assets and labels) so you can reuse them. They remain on the device unless you delete them, clear App data, or uninstall.
  • The App may use device motion sensors (for example the accelerometer) locally to help with signature-pad orientation. Sensor readings are not used for advertising profiles and are not sent to us as a separate telemetry stream.

3.3 App preferences stored on device

The App may store settings locally, such as theme, compression quality, favorite tools, folder labels, optional saved signature or stamp assets, ad-related usage counters needed for ad frequency, pending ad-opportunity flags, and whether Share Analytics is enabled. This preference data remains on the device and is not uploaded to an EdifyPDF document server.

3.4 Device permissions

Depending on the feature and platform, EdifyPDF may request:

  • Camera — to scan paper documents into PDF (optional; you can deny it and still use other tools).
  • Photos / media / storage — to import images or files and to save or share results (including saving images to your photo library where that feature is available).
  • Network access — to load ads, obtain advertising consent where required, fetch Remote Config, send Analytics / Crashlytics data (when enabled and configured), open web links and store pages you choose, request in-app ratings, and launch your email client for feedback. Document tools themselves are built to run on-device.

Advertising SDKs may also rely on platform advertising identifiers and related APIs (for example Android Advertising ID / Ad Services, or iOS SKAdNetwork). The App does not currently present a separate App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt; advertising consent, where required, is handled through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) and your device settings.

System file pickers may also let you choose files from cloud providers installed on your device (for example Google Drive). In those cases, the operating system and that provider handle access under their own policies; EdifyPDF only receives the file you pick for local processing.

3.5 On-device processing components

EdifyPDF uses third-party libraries and on-device components to provide features locally, including PDF rendering and editing engines, scan / alignment helpers, OpenCV-based image processing, on-device OCR (Google ML Kit text recognition; currently Latin script in production), and document conversion components (including LibreOfficeKit-based Word to PDF on supported platforms). These components process files on the device as part of the App. We do not use them to sell your document contents. Some Google on-device libraries may obtain or update models through Google Play services under Google’s terms.

3.6 Advertising (Google AdMob)

EdifyPDF uses Google AdMob to show full-screen interstitial ads in the free experience. Ads are typically considered after a successful tool operation when frequency rules say an ad is due — not while you are mid-edit. Exact timing can vary based on Remote Config, whether an ad is ready, and navigation timing (including short deferred display after you leave a tool flow).

To deliver, measure, and (where allowed) personalize ads, Google and its partners may process device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers where available), IP address, device/app information, approximate location derived from IP, and interaction data with ads. Where required by law, we obtain consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads. If you decline personalization, Google may still serve non-personalized or limited ads where permitted.

When required, Settings → Privacy may offer Ads privacy options so you can review or change your advertising choices. You can also reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings (for example Android ads settings or iOS Apple Advertising / tracking controls).

Google’s use of information for advertising is governed by Google’s policies. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3.7 Firebase services (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config)

Where Google Firebase is configured for your platform, the App may use Firebase services to operate and improve EdifyPDF:

  • Firebase Analytics — aggregated product usage, such as which tools are started, completed, or failed (using coarse reason categories such as password / cancelled / unsupported / unknown), an environment label (development vs production), and optional coarse source labels (for example home, tools, files, or viewer). Events are not intended to include PDF contents, passwords, full file paths, or document text.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — after a crash or certain unexpected failures, technical information such as stack traces, app version, device model, OS version, and privacy-safe breadcrumbs (for example tool name, processing stage, or coarse size buckets). Crash reports are not intended to include PDF contents, passwords, signatures, or full file paths.
  • Firebase Remote Config — configuration values such as monetization settings (for example whether ads are enabled, free operation thresholds, ad interval settings, ad unit identifiers, and product flags). Remote Config fetches do not upload your documents and are not controlled by the Share Analytics toggle.

Analytics and Crashlytics collection is on by default where Firebase is active, and can be turned off together in Settings → Privacy (“Share Analytics”). Remote Config and advertising may still use network access as needed to run the free App. Google processes Firebase data under its own terms. See Firebase privacy information.

3.8 Local diagnostic logs

The App may write technical log files on the device (for example app version, device model, operating system version, and error events) to help diagnose problems. Logs are retained locally for a limited period and are not automatically uploaded to us as a support inbox.

Development builds may include an “Export logs” option that lets a person manually share those files through the system share sheet. Production builds are not intended to show that developer export control.

Log entries are written with privacy in mind (for example avoiding device serial numbers). Still, logs may include technical details such as file base names or error messages. Do not share exported logs if they may contain sensitive names or context.

3.9 Feedback, ratings, and support

If you use “Contact us” / feedback or otherwise email us, your message is sent through your own email provider. We receive the content you choose to include, which may include your email address and message text.

If you choose Rate App, the App may use the platform in-app review API or open the Google Play / Apple App Store listing. Those flows are governed by Google or Apple.

3.10 Categories of personal information (summary)

Depending on how you use the App and which services are active, the following categories may be processed:

  • Identifiers — advertising identifiers, app instance identifiers used by Firebase/Google, and IP address (via Google services)
  • Device and app information — device model, OS version, app version, locale/region signals used by SDKs
  • Usage and diagnostics — tool usage events, crash diagnostics, ad interaction/measurement data
  • User content — documents and media you process, which remain on device unless you export/share them
  • Communications — support emails you send us

We do not intentionally collect government IDs, precise GPS location, payment card numbers, or biometric templates. Store billing for any future purchases is handled by Google or Apple.
