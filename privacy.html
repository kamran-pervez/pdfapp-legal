Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EdifyPDF (“EdifyPDF,” “the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use our mobile application on Android or iOS.

EdifyPDF is a PDF toolkit that helps you create, view, edit, convert, organize, and secure PDF files. Document processing is designed to run on your device. We do not require you to create an account to use the App.

1. Controller / contact

EdifyPDF is provided by an independent developer. For privacy-related requests, contact us at:

support.kklabs@gmail.com

2. Privacy highlights

  • PDFs, images, and related files you open or create are processed locally on your device for the feature you use.
  • We do not operate a EdifyPDF cloud service that uploads your documents to our servers for conversion or editing.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and the App does not show third-party advertising networks.
  • The App does not require a user login or EdifyPDF account.
  • Limited network access may be used when you open in-app links (such as this policy, Terms, store pages), send feedback with your email app, rate the App, or open a hyperlink inside a PDF.

3. Information we process

3.1 Your documents and media

When you use EdifyPDF features — including scan to PDF, images to PDF, import, view, edit, merge, extract, compress, rotate, reorder, delete pages, PDF to images, Word to PDF, password protect or unlock, sign, watermark, annotate, fill forms, and searchable PDF (OCR) — the App reads and writes files you select or create.

Those files stay under your control on the device and in storage locations you choose (for example Downloads, Files, or Photos). We do not use the contents of your documents for advertising or marketing profiles.

Temporary working files may be created while a tool runs and are cleaned up according to the App’s normal operation. Uninstalling the App removes App-private storage, subject to your operating system.

3.2 App preferences stored on device

The App may store settings locally on your device, such as theme, compression quality preference, favorite tools, folder labels, and optional saved signature or stamp assets you create. This data remains on the device and is not uploaded to a EdifyPDF server.

3.3 Device permissions

Depending on the feature and platform, EdifyPDF may request:

  • Camera — to scan paper documents into PDF (optional; you can deny it and still use other tools).
  • Photos / media / storage — to import images or files and to save or share results (including saving images to your photo library where that feature is available).
  • Network access — to open web links and store pages you choose, to launch your email client for feedback, and for similar user-initiated actions. Document tools themselves are built to run on-device.

System file pickers may also let you choose files from cloud providers installed on your device (for example Google Drive). In those cases, the operating system and that provider handle access under their own policies; EdifyPDF only receives the file you pick for local processing.

3.4 On-device processing libraries

EdifyPDF uses third-party libraries and on-device components (for example PDF rendering and editing engines, on-device OCR, scan helpers, and document conversion components) to provide features on your device. These components are used to process files locally as part of the App. They are not used by us to sell your document contents.

3.5 Local diagnostic logs

The App may write technical log files on the device (for example app version, device model, operating system version, and error events) to help diagnose problems. Logs are retained locally for a limited period and are not automatically uploaded to us.

Development builds may include an “Export logs” option that lets a person manually share those files through the system share sheet. Production builds are not intended to show that developer export control.

Log entries are written with privacy in mind (for example avoiding device serial numbers and similar identifiers). Still, logs may include technical details such as file base names or error messages. Do not share exported logs if they may contain sensitive names or context.

3.6 Crash reporting (not enabled by default today)

We may introduce an optional crash-reporting service in a future version (for example Firebase Crashlytics or a similar provider) to improve stability. If enabled, the App may send anonymous technical crash information after a failure — such as stack traces, app version, device model, and OS version.

Crash reports are not intended to include PDF contents, passwords, or full file paths. Before enabling such a service in production, we will update this Privacy Policy to describe it as active.

3.7 Feedback and support messages

If you use “Send Feedback” or otherwise email us, your message is sent through your own email provider. We receive the content you choose to include, which may include your email address and message text.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide and improve the PDF features you request
  • Save, export, print, or share files when you ask the App to
  • Remember your in-app preferences on the device
  • Respond to support or feedback you send us
  • Diagnose stability issues if crash reporting is enabled later

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your documents with advertisers.

Information may leave the App only when you take an action that does so, for example:

  • Sharing or saving a file with another app or cloud provider
  • Sending email feedback
  • Opening a website, store listing, or link inside a PDF
  • Manually exporting diagnostic logs (development builds)

Those destinations are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. If we enable a crash-reporting provider later, crash data will be processed by that provider under its policies for the purpose of crash analysis.

6. Retention

Documents, preferences, and local logs remain on your device until you delete them, clear App data, or uninstall the App (subject to OS behavior). Support emails you send are kept as reasonably needed to respond and for ordinary business records.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to an on-device utility app. No method of electronic storage is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, device lock, and PDF passwords you apply.

8. Children’s privacy

EdifyPDF is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information via email, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Your choices

  • Grant or revoke camera, photos, and storage permissions in system settings
  • Delete files you exported and uninstall the App
  • Avoid opening external links or sending feedback if you prefer not to contact us
  • Contact support.kklabs@gmail.com with privacy questions

10. International users

EdifyPDF is used on your device wherever you are located. If you contact us by email, the limited information in that message may be processed in the country where we or our email provider operate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or the App change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when we publish changes. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
